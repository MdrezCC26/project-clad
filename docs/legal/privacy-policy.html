Project Clad App

In addition to the Shopify storefront, we operate a companion application called Project Clad, which is operated by 11921029 Canada Inc. Project Clad is used exclusively by our own customers to manage projects, track custom part orders through production and delivery, and share fulfillment documentation with our staff. It is not offered as a product for other merchants to install, and it does not process personal information on behalf of any business other than our own.

What Project Clad collects

When you use Project Clad, we may collect and store:

  • Project, job, and order records, including line items, statuses, shipping or pickup choices, site contact details, and configuration details (such as dimensions, gauge, and colour);
  • Comments, approvals, member lists, share tokens, and related activity associated with your project;
  • Shopify customer identifiers, email (when provided by Shopify), customer tags, and company affiliation used to decide who can view or edit a project;
  • Delivery and fulfillment photos uploaded by you or our staff in connection with your order;
  • Purchase order documents (PDFs) associated with your order;
  • Files you attach to a cart line (for example drawings or part files), which Shopify holds briefly as staged uploads and which Project Clad then copies to our hosting provider;
  • Staff Shopify Admin authentication tokens needed for the application to communicate with Shopify. Your storefront password is stored by Shopify, not by Project Clad.

Where this information is stored

Project Clad's database and file storage are hosted with our infrastructure provider, Render, on servers located in Ohio, United States. If you are located in Canada or elsewhere, your information will therefore be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may have data protection laws that differ from those of your home jurisdiction.

How long we keep this information

Project Clad does not automatically delete or "age out" project data, photos, purchase order documents, cart-line files, or activity records after a set period of time. This information is retained until it is explicitly deleted or replaced, for example:

  • Delivery and fulfillment photos are removed or replaced when a photo is replaced with an updated one, when delivery records for an order are rebuilt, or when an order is moved back from a fulfilled or paid state to an earlier status in a way that clears fulfillment evidence. Deleting a project or job does not automatically remove the stored photo files.
  • Purchase order PDFs are removed or replaced only when a staff member explicitly deletes or replaces them. Deleting a project or job does not automatically remove those files.
  • Cart-line upload files are kept on our hosting until the related order data is deleted or replaced.
  • Staff Shopify Admin authentication records are retained until the app is uninstalled from our store or a staff member resets them; they are not deleted automatically.
  • Project activity logs are retained until the associated project itself is deleted. Deleting an individual order within a project does not remove that project's activity log.
  • Signed links used to view fulfillment photos or cart-line files expire after 90 days for security purposes. The underlying file is not deleted when a link expires. A new link is created the next time the relevant page is loaded and the file is requested.

If you would like us to delete information associated with your project, please contact us using the details below and we will action your request manually.
