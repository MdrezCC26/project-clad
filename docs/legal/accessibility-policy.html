Canadian Cladding is committed to providing our products and services in a way that respects the dignity and independence of people with disabilities. We are committed to giving people with disabilities the same opportunity to access our goods and services, and to benefit from the same services, in the same place and in a similar way as other customers.

Scope of this policy

This policy applies to all of Canadian Cladding's dealings with the public and with third parties in Ontario, including our storefront, our online store, our Projects ordering portal, quotations, deliveries and pickups. It is intended to meet our obligations under the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 (AODA) and the Integrated Accessibility Standards Regulation (O. Reg. 191/11), and applies to every employee, contractor, volunteer and agent who deals with the public or other third parties on our behalf.

Accessible customer service

We will communicate with people with disabilities in ways that take their disability into account. Our staff are prepared to serve customers by phone, by email, in writing or in person, and will adapt the way they provide information — including product specifications, dimensions, pricing and lead times — where doing so helps a customer complete an order or resolve an issue.

Assistive devices

People with disabilities are welcome to use their own personal assistive devices while accessing our goods and services. If a barrier prevents the use of an assistive device on our premises, we will work with the customer to find another way to provide the same service.

Service animals and support persons

Service animals are welcome in the areas of our premises that are open to the public, except where excluded by law. Where an animal must be excluded, we will explain why and offer another way to access our goods and services.

A person with a disability who is accompanied by a support person is welcome to have that person with them at all times while on our premises. We do not charge a separate admission or attendance fee for support persons.

Notice of temporary disruptions

If a facility or service that people with disabilities rely on is temporarily disrupted — for example an accessible entrance, an elevator, our online ordering portal or our phone lines — we will post a notice that explains the reason for the disruption, how long it is expected to last, and any alternative facilities or services available. Where practical, notices will be posted at the affected entrance, on this website and on our Contact page.

Accessible formats and communication supports

On request, we will provide information about our products, services, pricing and this policy in an accessible format or with communication support. We will consult with the person making the request about the format that works for them, provide it in a timely manner, and charge no more than the regular price we charge other customers for the same information.

Website and online ordering

We aim to align our website, product configurators and Projects ordering portal with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 Level AA. Practical steps include keyboard-operable forms and menus, text alternatives for meaningful images, labelled form fields for dimensions and gauge selection, sufficient colour contrast, and error messages that are announced in text rather than by colour alone.

Some content on our site is supplied by third parties, such as embedded media, payment and checkout pages, or supplier documents and drawings. We may not control the accessibility of that content. If you encounter a barrier anywhere in an order — including checkout — contact us and we will complete the transaction with you directly by phone or email.

Training

We provide training on Ontario's accessibility laws and on the Human Rights Code as it applies to people with disabilities to all employees, contractors and others who deal with the public or who help develop our policies. Training covers the purpose of the AODA and the requirements of the customer service standard, how to interact with people with various kinds of disabilities, how to work with assistive devices, service animals and support persons, what to do if a person with a disability is having difficulty accessing our services, and this policy. Training is provided as soon as practicable after a person is assigned duties, and again when this policy changes.

Employment and accommodation

We notify applicants and employees that accommodation is available during recruitment and throughout employment. When an applicant or employee requests it, we consult with them to arrange suitable accommodation in a manner that takes their accessibility needs into account, including accessible formats for job postings, assessments, employment information and workplace emergency response information.

Availability of this policy

This policy is publicly available on this website. A copy in an accessible format or with communication support is available on request at no additional cost, using any of the contact methods above.

Changes to this policy

We will modify or remove any policy, practice or procedure that does not respect and promote the dignity and independence of people with disabilities. This policy may be updated from time to time; the current version is always the one published on this page.
